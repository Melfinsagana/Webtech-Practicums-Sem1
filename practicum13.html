About the Placement Cell

The College Placement Portal is a single platform where students can explore recruiting companies, view available job roles, check eligibility rules, and register for placement drives.

Our placement cell works throughout the year to arrange pre-placement training, mock interviews and campus drives with reputed organizations.

Eligibility Criteria

Students must satisfy the following conditions to participate in campus placements:

  • Minimum 60% or 6.5 CGPA in the current degree
  • No standing arrears at the time of the drive
  • Minimum 75% attendance
  • Completed registration on this portal
