About the Placement Cell

The College Placement Portal is a single platform where students can explore recruiting companies, view available job roles, check eligibility rules, and register for placement drives.

Our placement cell works throughout the year to arrange pre-placement training, mock interviews and campus drives with reputed organizations.

Training Information

Before every drive, students take part in structured training programs.

Aptitude Training

Quantitative ability, logical reasoning and verbal ability practice with weekly tests.

Technical Training

Coding practice, database concepts and web development workshops handled by industry experts.

Soft Skills and Interview Preparation

Resume building, group discussions, communication skills and mock interviews.

Placement Process

  1. Student registers on the portal
  2. Company announces the drive and eligibility
  3. Pre-placement talk
  4. Online or written test
  5. Technical interview
  6. HR interview
  7. Offer letter released
